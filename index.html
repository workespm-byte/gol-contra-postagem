Gol Contra Postagem

Gol Contra Postagem is a private, internal tool used only by its owner to schedule and publish videos that he produces on his own channels: the Gol Contra YouTube channel and Instagram account (@golcontraoficial) and the Parallax View YouTube channel (@ParallaxViewYT).

It is not offered to the public and does not access third-party accounts. It only uploads videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, publishing times and a comment to the channels that belong to its owner.

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
